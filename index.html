The model
dN/dt = s − (kexp + knd)·N
dC/dt = kexp·N − kcd·C

At steady state:

N* = s / (kexp + knd)
C* = kexp·N* / kcd

Nuclear RNA relaxes at rate kexp + knd, so faster export or faster nuclear decay both make the nucleus respond faster. Cytoplasmic RNA lags behind, relaxing at kcd. Half-lives are ln2 / k.
